Ghosh et al. propose a factored sequence labelling approach for extracting methodology component names from AI research papers. They argue that methodology names are difficult to extract because they are large, fast-evolving, domain-specific, and context-dependent. Their work is closely related to the TechnicalMethod part of my project. However, my project focuses less on improving sequence labelling itself and more on organising observable methodology information into a small MethodologyProfile, including methods, datasets, tasks, and evaluation metrics.
